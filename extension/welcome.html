User Guidance

How SafeInbox AI Protects You

Designed with strict domain isolation so your inbox interface stays clean and fast.

01
📧

Open Any Email in Webmail

SafeInbox AI automatically activates when you open an email in Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo Mail. It inspects only the email message body and leaves all platform navigation, folders, and toolbars untouched.

02
🛡️

Dual Isolated Security Banners

Each email receives two distinct banners: Banner 1 (Top) audits hyperlinks and URLs only, while Banner 2 (Bottom) audits email text, brand authenticity, and manipulation cues without mixing.

03
🛑

Navigation Interception

Clicking a flagged phishing link triggers a safety confirmation warning modal. Safe clicks inside webmail are recorded in your local audit log (bounded to 500 items), while regular web browsing is never tracked.

04
📊

Native Side Panel Control

Click the extension shield icon to open the Chrome Side Panel. Run ad-hoc URL scans, inspect live email threat averages, review click histories, or connect to your cloud backend for global IOC sync.

Core Capabilities

Engineered For Zero-Trust Defense

Multi-layered client-side analyzers that detect sophisticated threat vectors before you click.

🔡

IDN Homograph & Typosquatting

Unmasks mixed-script Cyrillic and Greek character spoofing (e.g. paypa1 or goog1e) and measures domain edit-distance against protected enterprise brands.

Deceptive Redirect & Injection

General Web Search Opt-In

Privacy Commitment

Zero-Leak Privacy Guarantees

Built from the ground up to protect your privacy while defending your perimeter.

🔒

No Raw Email Retention

Neither the extension nor the cloud backend ever saves raw email text, sender addresses, or subject lines. Only cryptographic SHA-256 hashes are used for threat scoring.

🗂️

Strict URL vs Text Storage Separation

URL indicators and email text indicators are strictly partitioned across separate caches, storage keys, and database partitions with mathematical proof of zero cross-contamination.

🛡️

HTTPS-Only & TCP Network Enforcement

All backend and extension communication enforces HTTPS over TCP with 2-year HSTS pinning. Unencrypted plain HTTP is rejected client-side before any data leaves your browser.

Terms & Conditions Agreement

Please review and confirm the project's Terms and Conditions below. Once confirmed, your account will be created and your consent audit record will be logged.

1. Acceptance & Scope of Service

By confirming and creating your account, you agree to these Terms. SafeInbox AI provides client-side email threat inspection and hyperlink verification.

2. Autonomous Local-First Architecture

All core URL parsing, brand distance calculations, and ONNX inferences run directly on your device. The extension is 100% usable independently without backend servers.

3. Scoped Webmail Isolation

In Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo Mail, scanning is strictly confined to the active opened email message container. The email platform's navigation folders, sidebar chrome, toolbars, and avatars are never scanned or altered.

4. General Web Search Opt-In

External website scanning is strictly opt-in via a consent prompt ("Always Inspect" / "Leave It"). No DOM inspection runs on unconsented sites.

5. Zero-PII Privacy Commitment

Neither raw email text, sender addresses, recipient addresses, nor personal subjects are ever stored on backend servers or persistent cloud databases. Only irreversible SHA-256 hashes are used for threat scoring.

6. Strict URL vs. Text Storage Partitioning

URL IOCs and Email Text heuristics are maintained in completely separate storage keys and database tables. Training and testing datasets have zero data leakage (assert_zero_leakage).

7. Network Transport Lockdown

All cloud communications strictly enforce HTTPS over TCP with 2-year HSTS pinning. Unencrypted plain HTTP is blocked.

8. Consent Audit Logging

Upon clicking confirm, the system records your Email ID, Client IP address, Device/MAC hardware status (N/A Browser Sandbox Restricted under W3C policy), Terms Version 1.0.0, and UTC Timestamp.

9. Advisory Security Heuristics

Risk scores and threat warnings are advisory tools designed for defense-in-depth and do not guarantee 100% detection of unknown zero-day attacks.

10. Acceptable Use

You agree not to use the service for reverse-engineering heuristic rulesets for offensive weaponization, DoS attacks, or unauthorized exploitation.
